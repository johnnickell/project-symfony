  retain historical planning outcomes unchanged.

  ignores, upstream-behavior duplication, product tests of tools/wrappers/configuration/CI/test infrastructure,
  deliberately invalid fixtures, new production routes for tests, dependency changes, runtime expansion,
  authentication, persistence, browser suites, release and publication. Do not redo TASK-00007's presentation
