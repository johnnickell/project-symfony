# Fight Common Version Adoption and Support Evidence

## Problem Statement

The starter must prove its installed Fight Common support claim through its own Composer graph and booted Symfony
composition. A version constraint alone cannot prove the resolved candidate or lifecycle behavior.

## Solution

Adopt the compatible Fight Common `dev-develop` VCS candidate pinned in the lockfile, then record the canonical
receipt at `evidence/framework-support/receipt-v1.json`. Keep the future 2.0 migration visible but non-executable.

## Implementation Decisions

- The lockfile, installed candidate identity, selected capabilities, and booted journeys are the adoption evidence.
- The starter owns its framework-native configuration; Fight Common remains a Composer dependency only.
- 2.0 may not define speculative breaking changes before Fight Common publishes its owning migration authority.

## Testing Decisions

- Run lowest and latest compatible resolutions, booted Symfony journeys, receipt canonicalization,
  `./bin/planning-check`, and `./bin/build` before committing a passing receipt.

## Out of Scope

- Copied Fight Common source, a nested starter application, central cross-repository builds, releases, and publication.
